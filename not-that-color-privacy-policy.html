NOT THAT COLOR

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Not That Color (the "Game"), a local 2-player reaction game. We value your privacy and are committed to maintaining a secure and transparent experience for all players.

Strict Privacy Standard: The Game is fully offline, does not connect to the internet, and does not collect, store, share, or transmit any user data or personal information.

2. Data Collection & Usage

Because the Game functions entirely locally on your device, we do not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • In-Memory Scoring: Gameplay scores, player settings, and reaction times are processed completely in the device's temporary volatile memory (RAM) and are deleted immediately when you close or restart the Game.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not track your behavior, usage statistics, or device identifiers.

3. Device Permissions

The Game does not require or request access to any sensitive permissions on your device, such as your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, location, or network access.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game does not integrate any third-party services, APIs, advertisement networks, analytics platforms, or social networks. No data is shared with or sent to any third party.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is completely safe for players of all ages, including children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any data, there is no risk of collecting information from children.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the Game is completely offline, any policy updates will be published alongside any new versions of the Game on the app stores.
